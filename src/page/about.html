Our mission

We make EV charging easier to find, easier to access, and easier to share. VoltMap connects drivers with reliable charging wherever their journey takes them, while helping more people take part in the shift toward cleaner transportation. We’re building a connected charging network that makes going electric simple, accessible, and ready for everyone.
